## Motivation

Why does ReasonFlow exist?
Modern agentic AI applications are often tightly coupled to orchestration frameworks, LLM providers, and infrastructure choices. This makes them difficult to evolve, test, observe, and migrate as the ecosystem changes. ReasonFlow aims to provide a stable runtime abstraction that separates application logic from orchestration, enabling production-grade autonomous systems that are portable, observable, testable, and extensible.
